## Status, priority and dependency notation

- **Implemented (IM)** means source exists. **Deterministic (DV)** means the linked
  record contains automated evidence, possibly historical. **G-open**, **HW-open**
  and **H-open** mean graphical, hardware/real-environment and human acceptance remain.
  **Deferred (F)** is retained future work; **Superseded (S)** preserves a replaced
  requirement/interpretation. None means production certification.
- **P0:** authority, privacy, safety and data-integrity invariants; act first on a
  demonstrated breach, rather than opening a new generic security programme.
  **P1:** core usability and living identity, including long-standing artistic and
  conversation requirements. **P2:** important follow-on controls/platform/product
  work. **P3:** later expansion or alternative identities. Age, repeated owner
  emphasis and duration of deferral count after severity and dependencies; easier
  new work does not silently displace an older P1 requirement.
- Tracks: **C** conversation/speech; **V** visual identity; **U** usability;
  **A** authority/capabilities; **P** platform/distribution; **G** governance/docs.
  These are assignment families, not new engines or simultaneous task grants.
- Gate dependencies: **SCOPE** = a new bounded HQ assignment; **RENDER** = authorized
  graphical comparison; **AUDIO** = authorized physical/recognizer evidence;
  **REAL** = representative provider/device environment; **OWNER** = scarce owner
  acceptance; **DIST** = distribution/signing/clean-host gates. Requirement IDs in
  dependencies refer to applicable foundations/constraints in other rows, not a
  demand to complete all of their human acceptance before independent source work.
  Missing evidence does not freeze independent
  safe source work. Original board recovery is not a routine blanket blocker.

## Reconciliation, supersession and known omissions

- **Recovered, not newly invented:** UX-11 PTT, CONV-07 chunked speech and CONV-08
  selectable interruption modes are explicit early specification requests. Current
  source search found a keyboard microphone toggle and core modes, which do not
  complete those user-facing requirements. They deserve scope decisions rather
  than another generic “voice polish” entry.
- **Registered distinctly:** eleven STYLE IDs retain lava, optional richer/rainbow,
  wireframe, smoke/cloud, fractal, paint, synthwave, dark, gold, HAL-like and future
  identities. Palette/material experiments and structural alternatives have
  different probable costs; no theme-engine dependency is imposed in advance.
- **Implemented is not missing:** Memory CRUD/local store, bounded Console, Surface
  Flow, explicit en/es, basic diagnostics and provider cleanup have source evidence.
  Remaining usefulness, appearance, physical timing and broader compatibility are
  gaps, rather than permission to rebuild those foundations.
- **Unattributed symptoms remain:** false turns, apparent deafness, poor natural
  bilingual recognition, cold-start/typed stalls, room self-echo and brief speech
  response cannot be closed by V-A/V-B/IV-C's related deterministic repairs.
- **Superseded interpretations:** fixed warm-only allocation and old dome/four-draw
  counts are historical; later regional banks and IV-B supersede implementation,
  without accepting IV-B's art. Historical Linux-first MVP planning does not describe
  today's Windows-first validation. Conservative acoustic policy supersedes unsafe
  “VAD alone immediately owns interruption”; original latency ambitions stay unmet.
- **Proposals, not recovered demands:** 5–10× particles, centre wandering, semantic
  waveform effects, a particular solver/theme architecture or a specific future
  diarization/desktop framework are engineering suggestions until separately chosen.
  Do not inflate them into older owner requirements or silently implement them.
- Missing original transcripts/board and exact style dates remain U. Existing
  documents cannot establish hidden discussions, human acceptance or remote history
  beyond the bounded inspected snapshot. Future discoveries should add provenance
  to stable IDs, not erase conflicting versions or invent quotations.
