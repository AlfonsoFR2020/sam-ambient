## Stage 1 — optical hierarchy

The sheet silhouette/curvature is established. Dense medium pigment contrast and
similar body/skin light response camouflage it; neutral geometry is clearer than
full color. A first roughness/backlight-only trial still showed that problem.

The selected treatment uses the same densities/pigment anchors. Lifted skin
softens medium density and palette contrast, moderately reduces chroma and absorbs
some diffuse light. The hinge preserves the original field. Front satin, broader
quieter underside reflection, actual backlight and grazing response separate the
folds; bounded contact-pocket darkening supports attachment. Opacity, geometry,
draw count, radius response and field coordinates are unchanged. No outline/halo.

High additionally shades bounded **local ambient contact occlusion**, using the
same six seeded descriptors and current curl law. The spherical carrier inverse
and one tangential-curl correction locate the footprint; separation controls its
strength. Overlaps use max, not an accumulating dark multiplier. It is not a
light-space shadow, refraction, thickness simulation or exact soft transparency.
Low/Medium keep economical local material cues without the six-sheet contact loop.
Uniform descriptors add 384 bytes; no new draw, texture, framebuffer or mesh.

Compare body-only/full/neutral at yaw 0 / 0.75 / 1.6 and curl times 0 / 6 / 12 s,
holding body/light/color clocks fixed. The material-only/contact split was also
inspected; the calmer lifted pigment treatment provides the main readability gain.
The contact cue supports depth rather than justifying a brighter isolated render.

110 focused visual tests, TypeScript and four headless/sandboxed cases pass;

## Stage 2 — one spatial crest, one nearby-field response

Two related interventions use the existing wrapped ripple clock and measured,
freshness/owner-checked pulse. A shared world-space crest crosses the body and
raised skin; the lifted film receives a restrained broad glint. Near particles
meet that same crest later according to radius. Spread, size and opacity respond
locally rather than brightening every particle equally. No new oscillator,
semantic signal, model/microphone event, radius gain or geometry is introduced.
Listening light is concentrated toward the front of the receptive posture; this
is an illustration, not microphone localization. Existing internal thinking
circulation and independent idle clocks remain. The membrane's curl response,
Audio Reactivity zero, Reduced Motion, stale-event rejection and cancellation
continue through the established adapter/motion owners.

At the fixed 760 ms synthetic emphasis frame, radius remains 1.1154, the same
Identity II response. The contact sheet shows a stronger speaking composition,
subdued receptive contraction and internal working tension. Lighting/particle
changes are modest compared with the body pulse; naturalness remains provisional.
The crest is a visual grammar, not simulated acoustic propagation or understanding.
