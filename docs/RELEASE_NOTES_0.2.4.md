# Sam 0.2.4 experimental alpha — candidate notes

Local candidate, not published or approved. Windows-first incremental alpha;
typed conversation is the recommended dependable path, voice remains experimental.
This is not production certification or human visual acceptance.

## Everyday improvements since 0.2.3

- Correlated turn/generation recovery, complete role-separated answers, independent
  history scrolling and separate stopped-speech metadata.
- Conservative candidate ownership, empty-final rejection and protection against
  VAD-only cancellation of typed work. Common Markdown is normalized only for
  speech; original formatting stays in history.
- Cold model-inventory recovery, confirmed exact idle unload/reload and bounded
  cleanup that preserves externally owned services. LM Studio desktop closure is
  not promised by serving-endpoint cleanup.
- Persisted recognition language, Surface Flow and particle amount, effective
  voice/health reporting, clearer Recognition selection and sticky Controls tabs.
- Sandbox-enabled private owner app window, fullscreen and actual window sizing.
- Revised living pigment, form/membrane and measured listening/speaking response;
  these numerical changes did not satisfy the owner's October 9 visual judgment.

## Experimental foundations included

Owner-authenticated typed bounded capabilities, restricted workspace Console,
isolated public-page reading and structured proposal/policy mediation; local
SQLite reviewed personal memory with provenance, correction/deletion and bounded
lexical recall. These are experimental assets, not permission for unrestricted
shell/browser execution or automatic trusted learning from model/web text.

## Known limitations

Intermittent unsolicited/noise/self-playback voice turns and delayed responses
were reported. Contributing defects are fixed, but every intermittent cause is
not resolved or reproduced. Explicit Stop speaking and typed recovery remain
important. No production AEC or prompt full-duplex acoustic ownership is claimed.
Owner Spanish/English recognition remains poor in some conditions; installed
voice persona/pleasantness and real-room interruption are limited. Major STT,
visual/artistic Orb, refined persona settings and Console/Memory presentation
remain later work. No second beta is required for the deterministic fixes.
