## Owner journey

1. **Start and first impression (≈1 minute).** Use `Start Sam.cmd`. Can you tell
   whether discovery, loading and confirmed active model differ? Does the Orb look
   like a soft living object rather than a faceted/textured ball? Watch without
   touching for several seconds: do pigment territories evolve and palette
   relationships change locally? Can you see a restrained attached/lifted skin?
2. **Appearance and composition (≈1 minute).** Open Controls → Appearance. Change
   Surface Flow low/default/high: does material flow change while body movement
   stays independent? Briefly hold/drag the Orb: does material slow smoothly?
   Particle amount zero/default/high should change density. Restore preferences.
   Check an ordinary window and wide/fullscreen; history/Controls/diagnostics must
   be reachable and scroll separately. Check Reduced Motion only if comfortable.
3. **English and Spanish conversation (≈2 minutes).** Select English, speak one
   ordinary question, then Spanish and two short requests in your normal accent.
   Were beginning/end words captured? Did Sam answer each turn? Did the voice keep
   a coherent persona? Is it pleasant enough for everyday use? Observe whether
   listening feels receptive and speaking pulses follow emphatic syllables with
   expansion/light, rather than merely a binary speaking animation.
4. **Interruption and recovery (≈1 minute).** During a complete spoken answer,
   click Stop speaking. Playback must stop while the whole answer remains in
   history with separate “Speech stopped” metadata. Type a new short request.
   Optional natural overlap is an observation of the **conservative limitation**,
   not a promised ≈1-second barge-in feature. Sam must not manufacture YOU content
   from its own echo. Do not spend the session tuning/repeating acoustic overlap.
5. **History and model operations (≈1–2 minutes).** Read an older message while a
   new reply arrives; it should not pull you back down. Check clean YOU/SAM roles,
   emphasis/lists and complete content. While idle, unload the active model in
   System, verify the unloaded state, then load the selected exact model and make
   one text request. Rescan should preserve the confirmed route. Failed operations
   must leave retry/Rescan/Quit available, not falsely claim success.
6. **Quit (≈30 seconds).** Check cleanup applicability/preferences, then Quit.
   Engineering verifies actual provider/model state afterward: only the configured
   owned resources should be cleaned up. Externally reused service stays running.
