## Source checkpoint and hypothesis

The owner explicitly rejects V-F3's leaves/patches/ears. The actual repository
`sam-logo.png` supplies long counter-sweeping forms, not an invented concept board.
V-F4 tests three unequal **polar-rooted longitudinal trajectories**, two north and
one south, with unequal and opposing partial helical sweep. It preserves parent,
radial and V-F3 strategies; `living-connected` remains ordinary default.

Previous representations emphasized holes/collars, sector windows, then local
elliptical positive disks. This experiment replaces those charts with long paths:
theta = .17 + length*t; azimuth = origin + sweep*t + curve*t*(1-t), t in [0,1].
The centerline derivative supplies an orthonormal tangent/side frame. A variable
geodesic cross-section has angular half-width .035 + width*4*t*(1-t)*(1-.25*t).
Finite tapered ends avoid collapsed triangles. These are three topological disks
attached to the same living body, not a globally connected film or six old straps.

A shoulder q²/(.18+.82*q²)*(1-.15*q), q=max(0,(t-.08)/.92), gives C1 attachment and persistent
positive radial lift beyond the root. Radius-scaled backward longitudinal bend
opens the path; unequal lift, bounded transverse-section rotation and opposing sweep allow
over/under composition. No collision or exact inter-surface ordering is claimed.
The geometry is not an inextensible physical orange peel or a cloth simulation.

## Explicit parameter/material boundary

Each disk occupies a disjoint unit-direction atlas chart in the existing vec3
buffer. Its parameter direction is **not** a pigment direction. The closed strategy
contract supplies `bipolarMaterialDirection`, which maps it to the original body
direction before shared pigment sampling. Body displacement is evaluated at that
same material direction; the skin adds scale-relative lift/bend/twist. Overlapping
physical paths cannot accidentally sum another peel's deformation. No extra vertex
attribute, buffer, renderer, dependency or persisted appearance setting is added.

Complete-map finite-difference normals use the same .003 step on CPU and GPU.
Range-reduced degree-17 sine / degree-16 cosine series avoid the already measured
hosted nonunit trig-pair problem; independent native-trig error is bounded by CPU
tests. The strict GPU .0001 position / .005 normal gates are unchanged. The body
contact proxy follows the actual polar roots on High, rather than old openings;
it is ambient contact, not cast shadows or a general overlap solution.

## Expression and authority

The strategy consumes the existing membrane phase and smoothed measured output
pulse, work and receptive posture. Optional CPU pose fields map existing provider
loading/settling uniforms; older strategies ignore them. No new clock, audio
accumulator, event owner or inferred progress is introduced. Integer phase harmonics
preserve wrap continuity. Nominal body radius scales formation/compact/departure.
Idle stays lifted; speech modulates an existing form. Quiet/silent intervals release
the acoustic contribution while autonomous shape remains alive.

The CPU integration regression drives reducer → visual adapter → MotionEvaluator
with 24 seconds of approved synthetic PCM-derived levels, delayed acceptance,
silence, quiet late syllables, cancellation and stale callbacks. Zero gain, Reduced
Motion and hidden suspension are checked on actual candidate point displacement.
This is control/geometry proof, not natural-speech accuracy or acoustic timing.
